Equivalized household disposable income quintiles

This page has been archived on the Web.

Once each SPSD/M and SHS household has been assigned a household disposable income value as described in Income mapping, the equivalized disposable income is calculated by dividing each household’s disposable income by the number of consumption units of that household. Households are then grouped into equivalized household disposable income quintiles, which are calculated taking into account SPSD/M and SHS household population counts.
