# Owner-occupied dwellings split of L (2026-10-25)

Branch `feature/ooh-split`, built on `feature/sut-balance` (d847fb2). Not pushed. This is an input for the housing cash/noncash closure (validation SG report, section 3, option 2). It is additive: no existing canonical or compatibility file changes. The compatibility file hash is still `41590fdb…89aa`, as CRLF; the git LF blob is `f0004ad3…adb9`.

## Source

- StatCan provincial SUT, industry **BS5311A0 Owner-occupied dwellings**, mapped wholly into OECD-50 L.
- The table is symmetric, so the industry is also the commodity. Every province sells 100% of its output to HFCE **PEC04200 (imputed rentals for housing)**. Nothing goes to other uses, interprovincial trade or exports.
- The column contains:
  - intermediate inputs: repairs (BS23D000), FISIM, insurance, real-estate agents and so on;
  - net product taxes on those inputs;
  - taxes on production, mainly property tax;
  - gross mixed income, which is imputed net rent plus CFC on dwellings.
- There are no wages, social contributions or GOS.

## Outputs (`canonical/`, CAD millions, basic prices)

- `ooh_by_province_basic.csv`, one row per region:
  - OOH output and its sales to imputed rent;
  - inputs, net product tax on inputs, and VA components;
  - L totals including and excluding OOH, where `L_market_* = L − OOH`.
- `ooh_inputs_by_province_commodity_basic.csv`: OOH inputs by commodity and source (own-province, interprovincial, international).
- New hard S8 control `checks.ooh_split`. In every region the OOH row equals its column, the max gap is 3.5e-5 k$, and the source split sums to the BasicPrice column.

## Implications for the model (validation side; not done here)

1. **Household cash budget.** Owners' cash outlays for their dwelling are 66.6B: inputs 29.2 + product taxes 2.0 + property tax 35.4. The other 169.3B of the 235.9B imputed rent is noncash, with matching noncash mixed income. Mortgage interest is property income and is not in this column.
2. **The L firm sector is overstated.** In the model, the IO L industry carries OOH output and its 169.3B of mixed income as firm revenue and surplus. Under the SNA it belongs to owner households, as noncash income. Market L is 169.0B of output and 100.0B of VA.
3. **Dwelling CFC is not available here.** The 36-10-0096 extract is non-residential. Splitting mixed income into net imputed rent and CFC needs a residential CFC source, such as the residential series of 36-10-0096 or 36-10-0098. That source has not been fetched, and no number is assumed.
